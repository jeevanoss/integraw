## Project overview

Azzardo is one of Poland's leading premium LED and designer lighting brands. Their official online store serves thousands of customers across Poland and Europe with a catalogue spanning architectural lighting, track systems, decorative lamps, and accessories.

## What we did

- Built and integrated **custom PrestaShop modules** tailored to Azzardo's product configuration needs, including a track-light system configurator
- Executed a full **data migration** — transferring the entire product catalogue, customer records, and order history with zero data loss
- Carried out **performance optimisation** across the storefront, improving page load times and Core Web Vitals scores
- Worked closely with the Azzardo team to ensure the custom modules aligned with their fulfilment and catalogue workflows
