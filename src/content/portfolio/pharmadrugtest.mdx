## Project overview

PharmadrugTest is a French e-commerce platform selling professional drug-testing kits and related products to both individual consumers and professional organisations across France and Europe.

## What we did

- Architected and configured a **PrestaShop multistore** setup to serve different customer segments and regions from a single backend
- Developed **custom modules** to handle the specific compliance, product configuration, and ordering requirements of pharmaceutical product sales
- Integrated the multistore with appropriate payment and shipping solutions for the French and European market
