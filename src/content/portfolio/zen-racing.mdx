## Project overview

Zen Racing is a UK-based retailer specialising in remote-control cars, motorsport parts, and accessories. They needed a complete e-commerce presence built from the ground up.

## What we did

- Set up and configured **hosting infrastructure** optimised for PrestaShop
- Built and launched the full **PrestaShop store** — theme, catalogue structure, payment and shipping configuration
- Developed **custom modules** to handle product-specific requirements for the RC and motorsport category
- Delivered a complete, production-ready store ready for day-one sales
