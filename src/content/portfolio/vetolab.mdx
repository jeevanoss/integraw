## Project overview

Vetolab is a French e-commerce platform specialising in veterinary medical products and diagnostic solutions, serving veterinary clinics, labs, and animal health professionals across France.

## What we did

- Developed **custom PrestaShop modules** tailored to the specific requirements of veterinary product sales — including professional buyer verification and product-specific compliance features
- Ensured the modules integrated cleanly with the existing store and checkout flow
- Delivered a solution suited to B2B professional purchasing in the veterinary medical sector
